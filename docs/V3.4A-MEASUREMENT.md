## Measurement definitions and clocks

- Prior noise cutoff is the original reaction candle's opening time. ATR1m/ATR15m are the arithmetic mean of the last 14 true ranges; TR = max(high-low, abs(high-prior close), abs(low-prior close)). RV is RMS of the last 20 log close returns; its price unit is last baseline close × RV. TR noise is mean of 20 true ranges. Return/TR count, mean, RMS, population SD, min/max, first/last sample times and cutoff are persisted. Missing/noncontiguous samples are unavailable. Completed reaction/future bars are excluded. This SMA ATR definition is intentionally explicit; it is not mislabeled Wilder ATR.
- A bounded background cache covers at most 120 symbols × 80 minute bars, fetching four symbols per pass via the existing low-priority research scheduler. No decision or V2 scan awaits it. Early decisions can therefore have unavailable ATR1m/noise. Completed 15m control inputs provide ATR15m even during minute-cache warmup. Transport captures Bybit response-envelope server time and physical receive time without altering original public/private return values. Server time is not an individual exchange quote-event timestamp.
- Quotes preserve bid/ask, mid, absolute/bps spread, source/receipt/capture times, age and precision. Only snapshots received before the relevant decision/fill and no more than 120s old are available. Four bounded public-ticker snapshots per symbol permit pre-fill as-of quotes; later quotes are never backfilled. Depth remains null. A cached pre-fill quote is not a tick-exact quote at execution.
- Stop distance is abs(entry-structural invalidation), normalized by price, ATR1m, ATR15m, RV price units, mean TR and spread. Zero/unavailable denominators yield null. Decision and fill entry decompositions separate quote/open, adverse configured slippage and tick rounding; signed decision-to-fill market/total movement, stop/target gaps and geometry recheck are recorded. Fill baseline noise excludes the original reaction; ATR15m is explicitly the frozen pre-reaction decision baseline. Path retrieval after the modeled fill is marked historical reconstruction with its actual physical receipt time.
- MFE/MAE timestamp records are minute bounds, not tick timestamps. Original excursion values/precision remain unchanged. First raw and gross cost-risk touches of 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4 and 5 R use completed minutes. Nonterminal bars confirm touches. A target endpoint can prove a threshold was reached, without resolving intraminute order. Stop-terminal extremes remain a separate ambiguous channel; they never become confirmed favorable sequencing.
- Objective inventory is at most 12 ranked independently known opposing levels, content-addressed with immutable level definitions, causal state/knownAt/rank/reason, price/R/ATR/noise distance and selected-control marker. It is emitted when structural entry geometry exists; an absent reaction/entry has no meaningful ranked execution geometry. Relation to prior favorable extreme is exact from the existing dealing range. Preceding impulse is explicitly a confirmed-opposite-swing-range proxy, not a newly invented exact impulse.
- Management replay stores every evaluated filled minute OHLC, terminal flag, source/receipt time and stable chunk event ID in the `paths` channel. New active same-direction defended levels confirmed after fill are logged with knownAt and first physical receipt; the bounded checkpoint retains eight examples and a cursor. Structural progress is the first nonterminal completed 1m close beyond the frozen reaction zone. Hypothetical BE solves entry/exit taker fees plus adverse stop slippage and tick rounding at zero funding, and records the first closed-bar reach. Funding is explicitly excluded from this provisional BE measure. No management action runs.
- Premium/discount stays research-only with original range anchors, high/low/mid/percentile/source/knownAt and direction-relative zone. OB/POC metrics distinguish mere inventory presence from minimum zone gap/overlap, selected-level midpoint distance, ticks/%/ATR/noise, widths and freshness. VP remains OHLCV-bin approximation.
- Exact existing BOS/CHOCH records can be joined only by symbol/direction and causal knownAt/physical receipt. Their original event IDs and displacement definition are retained. An OB's exact frozen BOS identity is a fallback. Missing events/measurements stay unavailable; loose trend context is never relabeled an exact break event.
- Momentum uses direction-signed 3/12 completed 15m close returns (%); acceleration is signed last-3 return minus previous-3 return. Turnover/volume shocks divide latest completed bar by mean of preceding 20 excluding that bar. BTC strength is direction × regime sign × original positive strength; aligned BTC 3-bar return remains null if absent. Breadth uses the original snapshot with age and the exact valid non-BTC shared-bar symbol universe; legacy snapshots without that universe are unavailable in the new measurement channel.
- Groq/Alibaba observer records preserve candidate/episode, request/input hash/watermark, model/prompt/schema, completion/receipt/capture clocks, 120s TTL, status and causal availability. Explicit provider input watermark is preferred; request time is labeled an upper bound when the actual input watermark is absent. Continuation/exhaustion/confidence are copied only from structured returned fields; narrative is never parsed into them. Observer cache is bounded and may be unavailable after restart. AI never delays/overrides control. Alibaba configuration is unchanged. MI is explicitly `UNAVAILABLE_NO_LOCAL_CAUSAL_FEED`; there is no local causal MI provider feed to invent.

## Error, duplicate and storage contracts

New capture errors use the `errors` channel: timestamp, candidate/episode/symbol where known, subsystem, whitelisted code/message class, retry/skip, affected record and completeness flag. Raw exceptions, URLs, headers and provider responses are excluded; ordinary export redaction still applies. Bounded checkpoint summaries survive archive/disk/budget write failure. Legacy reported errors remain visibly unexported; no retrospective detail is fabricated.

Candidate-update IDs remain separate from stable episodes. Admission ordinal identifies repeated admissions. Filled-episode accounting is separate, with a bounded 512-entry dedupe index. All raw consumers still deduplicate stable event IDs: abrupt crash between append and checkpoint can repeat an event.

Capture remains `V3_COMPACT_V1` with additive versioned research definitions: `V3_MEASUREMENT_DEFINITION`, `V3_OBJECTIVE_INVENTORY_DEFINITION` and `V3_CONTROL_DEFINITION`. Join definition IDs within channel/hour. Dense candidate measurements that would exceed 8 KiB are preserved in a `FULL_DECISION_MEASUREMENT` definition, referenced by `measurement34.definitionId`, including all clocks and nested definition references. Age fields omitted from immutable definitions are derivable from current decision time minus knownAt. Derived noise/ratios use eight significant digits; quote/control prices, control geometry and clocks retain original precision. MARK-only records omit repeated decision/fill bodies; complete transitions retain them.

Limits stay 8 KiB candidate/AI rows, 32 KiB trade/path rows, 64 KiB raw gzip blocks, 1 MiB checkpoint, 32 active and 32 recent trades, streaming fixed-watermark exports, atomic small block replacement and whole-hour retention. The combined hourly envelope becomes **1.25 MiB**, hard cap **40 MiB**; 31 protected hourly budgets total **38.75 MiB**. This modest disk increase from 24 MiB is isolated to research storage, not strategy settings or the 352 MB Docker heap/512 MB service plan. Budget skips stay visible, never silently evicting protected hours. Legacy evidence is preserved separately.

Replay of 1,665 real V3.3 candidates over 1.4317 hours, actual matched V2/AI records and synthetic fully available measurement inputs, plus 32 one-hour research trades per hour with all minute paths, projected approximately **1.221 MiB/hour**, below 1.25 MiB/hour. This is workload projection, not a guarantee for all markets. Under the 64 MB heap replay, observed process peak RSS was about 88.3 MiB; deployment memory is checked separately. Final projection numbers are recorded in the handover.
