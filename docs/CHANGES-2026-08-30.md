Every change is commented in place with its evidence and reasoning. This file is the index.

## Deploy first, read second

Two things change live behaviour. Decide on both before you start the engine.

**1. Score normalisation shifts SCORE_BAND.** Both engines now report a true 0–100. TREND
scores run roughly 18% higher than before, so the band ceiling of 80 actually bites where it
was previously almost inert. Do not pool pre- and post-change trades in one analysis. If you
want the old behaviour back for continuity, revert the two `RAW_MAX` blocks (one in each
signals file) — they are self-contained.

**1b. The score was rebuilt and SCORE_BAND restored to 40/80.** An earlier draft of this pass
rescaled the band to 47/94 and recorded the score as "confirmed dead". Both were wrong and are
retracted — see the SCORE_BAND section of `docs/EVIDENCE.md` for the full correction.

Short version: score is U-shaped, so the mean test used to declare it dead was structurally
incapable of detecting it. And the band had never been tested anyway — all 19 closed trades
scored 66-78, with zero observations outside 40/80, because 37 points of every additive score
were constant (`rrPts` fixed at 15, `trendPts` pinned at 22 by a `Math.max` floor) and the one
component that varied had no journal column.

The score is now multiplicative on BASE 50, the architecture the band was originally validated
against, in both engines. `rrPts` removed. Every factor exported to the journal. Sweep range
10-97 with all three buckets populated, 80+ at 5.4% vs the 4.2% the old system observed. **If you
have 47/94 pinned from an earlier draft, reset it to 40/80.**

**2. Your stored overrides survive and still say 2.1 / 4.1.** The new defaults (3.0 / 5.0)
only apply to a fresh install or after a settings reset. Your instance keeps 2.1, which is
below the validated 3.0 floor. Change it deliberately or leave it deliberately, but know
which one you are doing.

## Critical

**C1 — SL_DISTANCE float boundary.** `gates.js`
The builders clamp the stop to sit exactly on a bound; the gate re-derived that distance from
the clamped price and the round trip lost a few ulps, so the comparison failed. Measured on
your 2026-08-29 journal: 14,523 rejections, **every one** sitting on a clamp, **zero**
genuinely out of range. Of signals clamped to precisely 2.1%, 36 passed and 14,293 failed.
73% of the signal population was discarded by arithmetic.
Fixed with a 1e-9 tolerance on both bounds. Verified: a stop clamped to exactly 3.0% passes;
1.5% and 9.9% are still rejected.

**C2 — BEAR_RANGE now blocked.** `signals_structure.js`
`regimeAllows` permitted shorts in BEAR_RANGE. Your only fully validated finding
(live t = −4.89, reproduced in backtest) was never in the code. Now returns `false` for both
sides. BEAR_TREND still permits shorts, unchanged.

**C3 — validated stop floor is now the default.** `settings.js`
`minSlDistPct` default 0.80 → **3.00**. A fresh deploy or a settings reset was silently
discarding the one fix you had actually proven. `maxSlDistPct` 2.50 → 5.00, purely so the
ceiling stays above the new floor — labelled in code as a placeholder, not a finding. Stale
fallbacks in both builders aligned.

## High

**H1 — score scales unified.** both signals files
TREND could reach 85, STRUCTURE 101, and one SCORE_BAND filtered both. The same gate was
strict for one engine and inert for the other, confounding every A/B you have run. Both now
normalise to 0–100. See the deploy note above.

**H2 — fake-BOS filter closed.** `signals_structure.js`
`hasAlreadyFailed` checked only the last 8 bars after the break. With the 30-bar staleness
cutoff, a break that failed at bar 3 and recovered by bar 25 passed cleanly. It was answering
"is this failed right now" instead of "did this ever revert" — not what your research
measured. Now checks every bar since the break.

**H3 — live + dual engines blocked.** `settings.js`
Dual mode allows the same symbol on both engines; Bybit one-way mode holds one net position
per symbol; `syncLiveTrades` maps positions by symbol alone. Fills and P&L would be attributed
to whichever local record matched first. Now forced off in `reconcile()` with an explanatory
message. Verified.

**H4 — journal writes batched.** `journal.js`, `server.js`
Every append rewrote the whole multi-megabyte array, and `recordBosEvent` can fire many times
per scan — so scan latency grew with history length. Now coalesced on a 3s timer with
`flush()` on SIGINT/SIGTERM. Verified: nothing on disk at append, flushed on demand.

**H5 — minLevelTouches actually works now.** `signals_structure.js`
Was an empty `if` block with a TODO, while the UI presented it as an active control with a
default of 2.

Two bugs had to be cleared to make it real. First, `keyLevels()` splits clusters by *current*
price — once a high is broken upward, price sits above it, so the broken level lands among the
supports and `levels.resistance` is the next untouched level beyond. Reading that gated on the
wrong level. Second, reusing `tolPct` (0.12–0.45%) as the "same level" test matched nothing at
all, so the gate silently never fired even after the first fix.

Now counts pivots within half an ATR of `brokenLevel` directly from `findPivots`. Verified on
a synthetic level measured at 10 touches: passes at `minLevelTouches` 1/5/10, blocks at 11/15.

**H6 — `entryMode` deleted.** `settings.js`
Single-option dropdown, never read anywhere. Pure dead code from before the dual-engine split,
and it was actively misleading you about which control selected the engine. The real controls
are `activeEngine` and `dualEngines`, both in the Engine group.

## Medium

**M1 — BOS tracker persists.** `bosTracker.js`
In-flight PENDING breaks lived in memory only, so every redeploy wiped them before they could
resolve — and you were redeploying to clear the kill switch. The dataset your best finding
depends on was the one most exposed to being lost. Unresolved events now save to disk
(debounced 2s) and restore on boot. Resolved events stay in memory for deduplication and are
pruned after 24h — dropping them early would let the same break be re-detected and
re-journalled, double-counting the exact statistic this module exists to measure.
Verified end to end: a break detected before a simulated restart survived and resolved to FAKE
after it.

**M2 — recovery buttons added.** `frontend/index.html`, `frontend/app.js`
`POST /api/control/release-kill` and `/clear-halt` both already existed on the server and were
never exposed in the UI. That is why clearing a stuck kill switch appeared to require a
redeploy, which is what cost you the 12 hours and the BOS data. Two buttons, both wired.

**M3 — duplicated regime logic removed.** `signals_trend.js`
The trend copy of `detectBtcRegime` did *not* drop the forming candle (the structure one does),
so it silently repainted — harmless only because nothing called it, but a live landmine. Its
`regimeAllows` had also diverged on BEAR_RANGE. Both now imported from `signals_structure.js`.
One rule, one place.

**M4 — extra candle slice removed.** `signals_structure.js`
`marketData.getCandles()` already drops the forming bar for every caller; slicing again dropped
a second genuinely-closed bar, leaving STRUCTURE permanently one bar staler than TREND. Not a
look-ahead bug — it erred safe — but the two engines were never judging the same data.

**M5 — long holds cannot skip a stop.** `executor.js`
The 1-minute fetch caps at 1000 bars (under 17h) while `maxHoldMin` allows 72h. Past the cap,
the earliest minutes fell outside the window and any stop or target hit in them was invisible —
the trade would drift to its time stop and book a price that never happened. Added a
`lastCheckedTs` watermark. Deliberately not persisted: it is a recomputable optimisation, and
losing it on restart just falls back to the old behaviour rather than forcing a disk write
every scan.

**M6 — fee accounting corrected, both sides.** `risk.js`, `executor.js`
*Paper:* both legs were charged on entry notional. The exit is larger precisely when the trade
won, so fees were understated on winners — biased in the flattering direction, on a system
where fees are close to deciding profitability. Exit leg now uses exit notional.
*Live:* `fees` was reconstructed from configured rates while `grossPnl` was set equal to
`netPnl`, so gross === net and `fees` reconciled with neither. Any fee-drag analysis on live
rows was meaningless. Now derived from Bybit's own `cumEntryValue`/`cumExitValue` so the three
numbers reconcile.

**M7 — `MAX_PER_ENGINE` visible.** `gates.js`
Recorded by the gate evaluator but missing from `GATE_ORDER`, so it never appeared in the UI
funnel — despite often being the binding constraint in dual mode.

## Deliberately not changed

- **`rrPts` dead constant.** Already documented in code. Removing it shifts the score's
  absolute scale, which now interacts with the H1 normalisation. Bundle it with the SCORE_BAND
  re-validation rather than changing two things at once.
- **`recentSwings` vs `findPivots` duplication.** Cosmetic. Two implementations of one concept,
  but they are not in conflict and consolidating them mid-validation adds risk for no gain.
- **Kill-switch auto-expiry.** With M2 shipped, the manual button is sufficient and an
  auto-clear could re-enable entries at a moment you did not choose.
- **Max drawdown as a percentage.** `summary()` reports absolute USDT off a zero-based curve.
  Fine at your current size, misleading once size varies. Worth doing before you scale.

## 2026-09-04 — LOCATION_RESEARCH_V1 (observational only)

Added a non-gating research telemetry layer to measure setup location inside the active trend.
It is intentionally isolated from scoring, gates, sizing, SL and TP so the existing strategy is
not changed while evidence is collected.

Recorded on every built signal (before gate evaluation) and frozen into every created trade:
- active impulse low/high/range and retracement depth at mark + planned entry
- deterministic location bucket and distance from directional extreme in ATR
- Bollinger 20-period basis/stddev/upper/lower, z-location and %B
- trend-leg proxy based on confirmed directional pivots since the latest EMA21/EMA55 trend cross
- deterministic Little-Rizzy research geometry: presence, sequence proxy, anchors, projected target,
  projected target in R, invalidation state and measured distance in ATR

The signal and trade JSON exports retain the complete `locationResearch` object. CSV exports flatten
all research fields into explicit columns. Existing historical trades are not backfilled; fields are
populated only for signals/trades created after this build is deployed.
